# Illustration sources

The three glyphs used by the current manuscript were generated with the built-in OpenAI image-generation tool. They are decorative representations of the named objects, not experimental evidence. The producer draws all text, states, and relationships as vectors. It crops the original PNGs without resizing or redrawing them.

## Selected prompts

### certificate-glyph.png

One neutral unbadged stack of two overlapping file cards, for a printed academic diagram icon. Flat crisp black ink outlines on a pure solid white background, exactly two cards, with three short horizontal lines on the front card. Small simple icon with no color beyond black and white, no gray glow or gradients, no badges, shadow, title, arrows or other objects. The plain white margin is very generous. The stroke should remain legible when the asset is scaled to 6 mm.

### manifest-glyph.png

Scientific journal artwork source on transparent background: a SINGLE flat orthographic manifest leaf, a nearly square record page with a small folded corner and two sparse ruled fields inside. Meant to repeat as the same object in a publication-order comparison. Crisp charcoal-navy ink, evenly weighted outline, no extra motifs, no gradients, no cards, no dashed background, no dividing rules outside the glyph, no numerals, text or arrows. Generous transparent margin.

### sqlite-glyph.png

One compact database icon only: dark black cylinder outline, top ellipse and a single curved inner seam, like a simple black engineering stencil on plain pure white. No glow, grayscale gradients, shadows, background texture, text, cards, internal symbols or arrows. Center with generous empty white margin. Minimal readable object at 6 mm print size.
